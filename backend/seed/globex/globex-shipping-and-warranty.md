# Globex Shipping and Warranty

This document belongs to a second, fictional tenant (Globex). It exists to demonstrate
and test tenant isolation: Acme users must never receive answers from it.

Globex standard shipping takes 9-12 business days. Standard shipping costs $19.00 for every order.

Globex devices include a 5-year warranty covering manufacturing defects.

## Refund Window

Globex accepts returns within 60 days of delivery.
