Account FAQ

Answers to common questions about managing your Acme account. All content is fictional.

How do I reset my password?

Select Forgot password on the sign-in page and enter the email address on your account. We will email you a password reset link. The reset link expires after 60 minutes.

How do I change the email address on my account?

Go to Account Settings, then Profile, and select Change email. We send a verification link to the new address, and the change takes effect after you confirm it.

How many shipping addresses can I save?

You can save up to 10 shipping addresses in your Acme account.

How do I turn on two-factor authentication?

Go to Account Settings, then Security, and select Enable two-factor authentication. Acme supports authenticator apps for two-factor authentication.

  • Scan the QR code with your authenticator app.
  • Enter the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.
  • Save your backup codes somewhere safe.

Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

You can change or cancel an order from Order History within 1 hour of placing it, as long as the order has not started processing.

How does Acme Rewards work?

Acme Rewards members earn 1 point for every $1 spent. Every 500 points can be redeemed for a $5 reward on a future order. Points expire 12 months after they are earned.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account, contact Acme Support. Account deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Order records are retained for 7 years to meet tax obligations.
