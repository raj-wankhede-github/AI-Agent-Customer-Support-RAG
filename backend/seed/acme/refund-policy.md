# Refund and Returns Policy

Effective date: 2026-01-01. This policy applies to purchases made on acme.example. All content is fictional.

## Return Window

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Items must be unused and in their original packaging, with all accessories included.

## How to Start a Return

1. Sign in to your Acme account and open Order History.
2. Select the order and choose Start a Return.
3. Print the return label and drop the package off with the carrier within 7 days.

## Refund Timing

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after our warehouse receives and inspects your return. You will receive an email when the refund has been issued.

## Return Shipping Costs

Return shipping is free for items that arrived defective or damaged. For all other returns, a return shipping fee of $6.95 is deducted from your refund.

## Items That Cannot Be Returned

- Gift cards
- Items marked as final sale
- Personalized or engraved products
- Software or digital downloads that have been opened or activated

## International Purchases

International purchases can be returned within 45 days of delivery. Customers are responsible for return shipping costs on international purchases. Original shipping charges, customs duties and import taxes on international purchases are non-refundable. Refunds for international purchases are issued in the currency of the original payment.

## Exchanges

Acme does not process direct exchanges. To get a different size or color, return the original item for a refund and place a new order.
